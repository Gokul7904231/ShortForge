# Hierarchy: Floor Auditors & Verification Specialists

> **Tier**: Verification & Compliance (Level 2/3)  
> **Location**: `apps/web/factoryos/core/contracts/` & `OverseerControlPlane.ts`

## 1. Responsibilities

Auditors perform independent, non-destructive verification passes on stage artifacts:
- **Floor 07 Auditor (`worker_auditor_01`)**: Validates the physical video container format, 9:16 aspect ratio, audio channel alignment, subtitle timing, and content policy.
- **Factual Auditor**: Verifies accuracy of generated statements against knowledge bases and source documents.
- **Economic Auditor**: Verifies token expenditure against user tier limits and budget caps.
