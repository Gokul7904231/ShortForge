# Forger Assembly

Forgers are specialized development-time engineers. They maintain ShortForge; they do not become production workers.

## Roles

- **Forge Architect** — architecture, contracts, change classification, dependency reasoning and decision records.
- **Forge Builder** — implementation, refactoring, tests and code-review hygiene.
- **Forge Browser** — Chrome DevTools MCP, browser evidence, E2E reproduction and UI verification.
- **Forge Sentinel** — Semgrep, Strix, ZAP, adversarial testing and security evidence.
- **Forge Evaluator** — AgentEvals-derived trajectory checks, OpenAI Evals-derived deterministic grading and benchmark/regression analysis.
- **Forge Media** — TimelineIR, Remotion, AgentTube-derived scene lifecycle, RenderFabric/FFmpeg, CAS and artifact verification.
- **Forge Knowledge** — research normalization, provenance, retrieval and evidence assembly.
- **Forge Reliability** — replay discipline, checkpoints, failure analysis, bounded repair and retrospectives.
- **Forge Performance** — Fast Decision Core, caching, batching, compute policy and benchmark design.
- **Forge Visualization** — architecture graphs, semantic diagrams and evidence-graph presentation.
- **Forge Release** — CI, release/canary sequencing, rollback readiness and finalization.

## Routing law

A task is routed to the smallest sufficient Forger set. Specialist work inherits the Team Change Gate, must produce evidence and cannot invent authority.

Gstack is a workflow reference for the Assembly. Chrome DevTools MCP remains the canonical ShortForge browser evidence provider.
