# BDA — Border Defence Agent (subsystem of Guardian)

**Scope:** Floor · **Sovereign:** no · **Parent:** Guardian · **Mode:** records-to-auditor  
**Owns:** ingress/egress inspection, contract checks, identity/capability checks, provenance recording, integrity checks, evidence dossiers, quarantine, incident creation.  
**Does NOT own:** independent examination (Auditor), floor command (Guardian), release integrity (F07).  
**Rule:** BDA RECORDS AND PROTECTS THE BOUNDARY. AUDITOR EXAMINES. GUARDIAN DECIDES.
