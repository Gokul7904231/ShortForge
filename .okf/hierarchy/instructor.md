# INSTRUCTOR — Knowledge Minister (per floor)

**Scope:** Floor · **Sovereign:** yes (role 10) · **Primary verb:** teach · **Reports to:** Guardian  
**Owns:** floor procedural knowledge, failure learning, training material, evaluation scenarios, Ascalon knowledge preparation, version-controlled operating guidance.  
**Does NOT own:** global security policy, operational authority, command.  
**Feeds:** verified experience → lessons → Ascalon → Guardian decision support.  
**Rule:** INSTRUCTOR TEACHES THE FLOOR HOW TO OPERATE CORRECTLY. LEARNING != PROOF.
